Our Story

Useful work, kept simple.

Lindrith Wood Co. began as a practical hobby: time outside, physical work, and the satisfaction of turning rough logs into something useful.

How it started

What began with a few tools and a growing woodpile gradually became a small local business. The work offered a welcome contrast to desk work—direct, tangible, and easy to understand.

The business remains intentionally modest. The aim is dependable service, careful preparation, and a personal relationship with the work.

More than firewood

This site also serves as a personal home for writing about family, dogs, rural New Mexico, projects, tools, data, and whatever else seems worth preserving.
